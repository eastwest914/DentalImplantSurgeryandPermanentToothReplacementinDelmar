Questions About Implants and Oral Surgery

What happens during a dental implant procedure?

A small titanium post is placed in the jawbone to act as a tooth root. After healing, a crown, bridge or denture attaches to it. We walk you through the stages in advance.


Why would I need bone grafting?

Implants need enough healthy bone to hold them. If bone has shrunk after tooth loss, grafting can rebuild it so an implant can be placed.


What is a sinus lift?

A sinus lift adds bone beneath the sinus in the upper jaw so there is enough support for implants. We explain whether it applies to your case.


How much do dental implants cost?

The cost depends on how many teeth you are replacing and whether grafting or other steps are needed. We provide a clear estimate after your consultation.
